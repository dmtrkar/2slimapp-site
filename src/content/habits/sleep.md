Sleep doesn't get talked about as much as food or exercise in most weight-loss advice, but it's rarely separate from either. A short night tends to be followed by a day where portions creep up and motivation to move drops — not as a moral failing, just as a pattern worth being able to see.

## A number you log, not a device you wear

You don't need a sleep tracker or a wearable to log this habit — just a rough sense of how long you slept, entered the same way you'd log a glass of water. Over time, that simple log makes the connection between a short night and a harder next day much easier to spot for yourself.

## Part of the same picture

Sleep sits alongside Nutrition, Water, Activity, and the Daily Weigh-in — five small logs that, together, tend to explain more about a hard day than any one of them could on its own.
