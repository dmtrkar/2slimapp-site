Some days the gap between your Nutrition log and how the day actually went isn't explained by hunger at all. Stress is 2Slim's optional sixth tracker — separate from the five core habits — for logging the emotional weather that often sits behind eating patterns the other habits alone don't explain.

## Five feelings, not a mood score

Instead of a single stress rating, the check-in logs five specific triggers — anxiety, anger, sadness, fear, and guilt — each with its own simple count for the day. Naming which one shows up is often more useful than a vague sense that "today was stressful," and it's what makes the pattern visible over time.

## Optional, and worth it on the right days

Stress won't show up on every day's log, and it doesn't need to. On the days it does, it tends to explain a lot — connecting a heavier Nutrition log or a skipped Activity entry back to something more specific than willpower.
