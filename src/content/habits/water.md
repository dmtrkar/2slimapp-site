Water is the easiest of the five habits to forget you're neglecting. There's no hunger cue for it, no obvious signal — just a slow drift toward the end of the day realizing you've had one glass since breakfast. Logging it in 2Slim turns that invisible gap into something you can actually see.

## A tally, not a target you dread

Each glass you log is a small, low-stakes tap — no measuring cups, no ounces to calculate. The point isn't to hit a rigid number every single day; it's to build a rhythm you notice when it's missing. A day with zero glasses logged by 3pm tells you something useful about how the day has gone, long before you'd otherwise think to ask.

## Where it sits alongside the other habits

Water sits next to Nutrition, Activity, Sleep, and the Daily Weigh-in on your Today screen — one glance shows you which habits you've kept up with and which have quietly slipped. It's a small thing on its own, but it's often the first habit that reveals when a day has gotten away from you.
